# Hosted Testing Service Acceptable Use

The hosted CMP relay is for lawful development and interoperability testing.
Do not use it to:

- violate law or another person's rights;
- harass, threaten, exploit, or impersonate others;
- distribute malware, phishing, spam, or abusive automated traffic;
- probe or attack accounts, infrastructure, rate limits, queues, or prekey
  inventories without explicit authorization;
- evade service limits through bulk accounts or coordinated clients;
- upload regulated or safety-critical communications;
- interfere with other users or the operation of the service.

The operator may throttle, suspend, delete, or block accounts and traffic to
protect the testing service, investigate abuse, comply with legal obligations,
or perform upgrades. There is no appeal or continuity guarantee during the
experimental period.

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Do not test a vulnerability against other users or live data.
